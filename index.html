01 / A little about me

Different arenas.
Same drive.

Curious by default. Competitive by choice. At home in a code editor, on stage, or at the starting line.

I’m Prince Darji, a Bachelor of Computer Applications student at Shree Swaminarayan Institute of Technology (SSIT), affiliated with Gujarat Technological University (GTU).

My world connects software, leadership, sport, the outdoors, and creative media. I build practical products, help bring campus tech events to life, compete across the track and court, guide trekking groups, and create visual stories as @unfilter_prince.

Each arena makes me better in the others: teamwork from sport, composure from competition, care and awareness from guiding, and perspective from community. Whether I’m building a smarter dispatch flow, welcoming a first-time hackathon participant, or leading a group outdoors, I bring the same focus: show up prepared, look out for the team, and keep moving forward.
